Discover the Clube de Palma story

A private member’s club on the Konkan Coast — recreation, belonging and timeless coastal living, inspired by Goa’s easy elegance.

The timeless story of Clube de Palma on the Konkan Coast

The revered sanctity of a Member’s Club lies in the unspoken relationships forged within its walls; the shared experiences blossoming into lasting bonds. It is the growing familiarity between two polite smiles, the fierce competition volleyed over a net, or the plate of chips shared after a swim. Amidst the sway of palms and the cool sea breeze, Clube de Palma is a promise of 40,000 sq.ft. of recreational bliss, welcoming individuals to build a beautiful community in the shade and vibrance of the Konkan Coast.

Spirit across the net

Fierce competition volleyed over a net under Goan light — then the easy walk back toward shared tables, where sport softens into friendship.

The heart of the Clube

From the pavilion, 40,000 sq.ft. of recreational bliss unfolds — terracotta roofs, still water and palm shade framing a sanctuary built for belonging on the Konkan Coast.

Hours that linger

Unspoken bonds blossom by the water — a raised glass, an easy conversation, the growing familiarity between two polite smiles that becomes the soul of club life.
