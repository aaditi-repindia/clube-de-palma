Clube de Palma is a private member’s club on the Konkan Coast, shaped around recreation, belonging and timeless coastal living.

Inspired by Goa’s easy elegance, the club brings together sport, wellness, dining and celebration — spaces curated for shared experiences and lasting bonds.
