The Concept

Clube de Palma is a private member’s club on the Konkan Coast, shaped around recreation, belonging and timeless coastal living.

Inspired by Goa’s easy elegance, the club brings together sport, wellness, dining and celebration — spaces curated for shared experiences and lasting bonds.

Membership

More than a place. A way of belonging.

Clube de Palma brings together like-minded individuals and neighbours to create a community. Members have exclusive access to everything our clube has to offer — recreation, sport, wellness, dining and shared celebration.

Access to club facilities

Enjoy the full recreational campus — pools, courts, indoor games, banquet and social spaces.

Sporting & wellness life

From racquet sports and gymnasium training to yoga in the garden, everyday rituals of wellbeing.

Dining & social privileges

Gather at Sabores, Café Verde and O Bar Azul — coastal evenings with members and guests.

Family experiences

Kids’ pools, play areas and guided sport lessons create a nurturing environment for all ages.

Celebrations & community

Host landmark moments in the banquet hall overlooking the central courtyard.

Membership categories and fees follow official club policy. Full applications are completed on the club’s existing membership system.
